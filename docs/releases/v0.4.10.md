### 修复 / Fixes

- **修复启动后点击菜单栏图标会弹出空白的“FanBar 设置”窗口** — 在较新的 macOS 上，系统会在启动时打开一个空的设置占位窗口，点击菜单栏图标时它会随面板一起出现。现在占位窗口不会再显示；在 FanBar 运行时再次打开 App 或按 ⌘, 会直接打开真正的设置窗口
  **Fix a blank "FanBar Settings" window appearing after clicking the menu bar icon** — On recent macOS releases, an empty placeholder settings window opened at launch and came forward with the menu panel. The placeholder no longer appears, and reopening FanBar while it runs or pressing ⌘, opens the real settings window
