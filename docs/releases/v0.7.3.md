### 修复 / Fixes

- **SMC 暂时拒绝风扇写入时自动恢复** — 睡眠唤醒后，SMC 有时会在几分钟内拒绝切换风扇模式。0.7.2 在连续失败 5 次后就把风扇交回 macOS，提示"需要重启 Mac"，并且不再尝试，用户只能手动切回原来的模式。现在风扇仍会先交回 macOS，随后 FanBar 在后台按 30 秒、1 分钟、2 分钟、之后每 5 分钟的间隔重试原来的模式，最长 1 小时；写入成功后自动重新开启。重试失败不会弹出错误提示，期间手动切换任何模式都会取消重试。只有 1 小时后仍被拒绝，才会建议重启 Mac (#131, #114)
  **Recovers on its own when the SMC refuses fan writes for a while** — after sleep and wake, the SMC sometimes refuses to switch fan modes for a few minutes. In 0.7.2, after five failures in a row FanBar handed the fans to macOS, said a restart was needed, and stopped trying, so you had to switch back by hand. The fans still go back to macOS first, but FanBar now retries your mode in the background after 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes and then every 5 minutes, for up to an hour, and turns it back on once a write gets through. Failed retries show no error, and picking any mode yourself cancels them. A restart is suggested only if the SMC is still refusing after an hour (#131, #114)

### 改进 / Improvements

- **支持入口更低调** — 面板中的「支持」按钮不再是醒目的实心按钮，不会盖过授权控制服务这类真正需要处理的操作。设置「通用」页底部的链接合并为一行 (#123)
  **A quieter support prompt** — the panel's Support button is no longer a bold filled button, so it no longer outranks actions that fix something, such as authorizing the control service. The links at the bottom of Settings → General now share one line (#123)
