### 新增 / New

- **设置窗口重新设计** — 原生偏好设置样式工具栏（散热 · 菜单栏 · 通用），散热预设改为卡片式选择，曲线卡片带温度来源、当前温度标记与目标转速、控制点检查器；高级选项（降温缓冲、每步最大变化）默认折叠
  **Redesigned the settings window** — a native preference-style toolbar (Cooling · Menu Bar · General), card-based preset selection, and one curve card with its temperature source, a live marker showing the target RPM, and a control-point inspector; advanced options (cooling buffer, max step) stay collapsed by default
- **首次启动引导新增控制服务授权提示** — 未授权时会提示"按需调整散热需要一次性授权"，并直接提供启用入口，不再让新用户第一次尝试调节转速时静默失败
  **The first-run onboarding now surfaces the control-service permission** — when it hasn't been granted yet, a notice explains that adjusting cooling needs one-time permission and offers to enable it directly, instead of silently no-oping the first time a new user tries

### 修复 / Fixes

- **修复温度曲线横轴跨整点丢失小时** — 时间格式从 `mm:ss` 改为 `HH:mm:ss`
  **Fix the temperature chart's x-axis losing the hour across an hour boundary** — the time format changed from `mm:ss` to `HH:mm:ss`
- **修复锁屏、休眠或重启造成的采样空洞被连成一条直线**，且平滑窗口会把断档两侧的读数混在一起；现在断档处正确断线，平滑也不会跨越断档。同时把最近十分钟的温度历史写入本地，重新打开 App 后曲线不再从空白开始
  **Fix a straight line being drawn across sampling gaps** left by sleep, a locked screen, or a relaunch, and the smoothing window blending readings from either side of the gap; the trace now breaks at the gap and smoothing stops at the boundary too. The last ten minutes of history are also persisted locally, so the chart no longer starts blank after relaunching
- **修复设置窗口切换 Tab 时的界面抖动** — 内容滚动条强制使用悬浮样式，不再因为"是否需要滚动"而改变内容区宽度
  **Fix the settings window's content visibly shifting when switching tabs** — its scroller is now forced to overlay style, so the content lane's width no longer depends on whether that tab happens to need scrolling
