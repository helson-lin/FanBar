### 功能 / Features

- **温控曲线锚点可拖过相邻锚点** — 在曲线图上拖动锚点不再被前后锚点挡住，松手后曲线按温度自动重排，该锚点保持选中且转速不变；落在已有锚点的温度上时会自动错开一格 (#47)
  **Curve anchors can be dragged past their neighbors** — dragging an anchor on the curve is no longer blocked by adjacent anchors; the curve re-sorts by temperature, the anchor stays selected and keeps its speed, and landing on an occupied temperature shifts it by one degree (#47)
