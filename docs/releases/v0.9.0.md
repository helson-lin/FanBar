### 新功能 / New Features

- **在菜单栏显示 CPU、内存和 GPU 占用** — 设置 → 菜单栏 → 系统资源，可以分别打开 CPU、内存、GPU 三个菜单栏项目，样式可选「名称与数值」或「图标与数值」，默认都关闭。点按其中一项会打开面板并突出显示对应的资源；面板已打开时点按另一项，面板会移到那一项下方。按住 ⌘ 拖动调整的位置会被记住 (#155)
  **CPU, memory and GPU usage in the menu bar** — in Settings → Menu Bar → System resources, turn on separate menu bar items for CPU, memory and GPU usage, in a Label and value or Icon and value style. All three are off by default. Clicking an item opens the panel and highlights that resource; with the panel open, clicking another item moves the panel under it. Positions you arrange with ⌘-drag are remembered (#155)

- **「记录」设置更易上手** — 原「日志」标签页改名为「记录」，顶部说明这项功能做什么，并显示正在记录、未开启或写入失败的状态，可以直接用表格打开、在访达中显示、重试或更换位置。新增三种预设：日常（每 10 秒，保留 30 天）、排查问题（每 2 秒，保留 7 天）、长期观察（每 60 秒，保留 90 天）；间隔、保留时间、文件方式、记录字段和位置放在高级设置里，并显示当前占用和预计占用的磁盘空间 (#155)
  **Simpler History settings** — the former Log tab is now History. Its top card explains what the feature does and shows whether it is recording, off or failing to write, with Open Spreadsheet, Show in Finder, Retry and Change Location… buttons. Three new presets: Everyday (every 10 s, keep 30 days), Troubleshooting (every 2 s, keep 7 days) and Long term (every 60 s, keep 90 days). Interval, retention, file policy, logged fields and location are under the advanced settings, along with the disk space used now and an estimate (#155)

- **自动清理旧记录** — 可以选择保留 7、30、90 天或永久保留。FanBar 每天一次把它自己写的过期 `FanBar Log*.csv` 文件移到废纸篓，正在写入的文件不会被动到。**已经在记录的用户请注意：** 如果从未设置过保留时间，升级后默认保留 30 天，超过 30 天的记录文件会在一天内移到废纸篓，可以从废纸篓恢复。想保留全部记录，请在 设置 → 记录 → 高级 中把「保留」改为「永久」(#155)
  **Old history files are cleaned up** — keep history for 7, 30 or 90 days, or forever. Once a day, FanBar moves its own expired `FanBar Log*.csv` files to the Trash; the file being written is never touched. **If you already record history:** when you never set a retention period, it now defaults to 30 days, so within a day of updating, history files older than 30 days are moved to the Trash, where you can restore them. To keep everything, set Keep to Forever under Settings → History → Advanced (#155)

### 改进 / Improvements

- **「通用」顶部显示控制状态** — 能控制风扇时，显示风扇数量，并说明断开连接、睡眠或写入失败时风扇会交还 macOS；控制服务需要处理时，显示原因和对应操作；读不到风扇时显示「读不到风扇数据」。各项标题更短，较长的说明移到了悬停提示 (#155)
  **General shows the control status at the top** — when FanBar can control the fans, it shows the fan count and that the fans return to macOS on disconnect, sleep or a failed write. When the control service needs attention, it shows why and what to do. When no fan can be read, it says "No fan data". Row titles are shorter, with longer explanations moved to help tags (#155)

- **菜单栏和面板设置分开** — 原「外观」拆分为「菜单栏」和「面板」两个标签页；面板中部的监控模块现在在「面板」中设置 (#155)
  **Separate Menu Bar and Panel settings** — the former Appearance tab is split into Menu Bar and Panel tabs; the panel's monitoring modules are now set up under Panel (#155)

- **写入记录失败后不再反复重试** — 写入失败后，依次在 1、5、15 分钟后重试，而不是每次采样都重试。点「重试」或更改设置会立即重新开始 (#155)
  **Failed history writes back off** — after a failed write, FanBar retries after 1, then 5, then 15 minutes instead of on every sample. Retry or a settings change starts again right away (#155)

### 修复 / Fixes

- **macOS 11 和 12 可以启用控制服务了** — 以前在 macOS 11 或 12 上启用控制服务时，输入管理员密码后仍显示未启用，所以无法控制风扇。现在控制服务会正确安装，重启后也会保留 (#157)
  **The control service can be enabled on macOS 11 and 12** — on macOS 11 or 12, enabling the control service used to show it as still off after you entered your administrator password, so FanBar couldn't control the fans. The control service now installs correctly and stays installed after a restart (#157)

### 注意 / Notes

- 记录的新文件默认每天新建一个，CSV 的列没有变化。
  History now starts a new file each day by default; the CSV columns are unchanged.
- 在 macOS 11 和 12 上，启用控制服务会请求一次管理员密码。之后升级 FanBar 时，可能需要再输入一次密码来更新控制服务。
  On macOS 11 and 12, enabling the control service asks for your administrator password once. Later FanBar updates may ask for it again to update the control service.
