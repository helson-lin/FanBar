## Design Context

### Users

FanBar serves Mac users who want a quick, trustworthy view of system temperatures and occasional control over fan cooling. They visit the site to understand what the native menu-bar utility does, verify that it is safe and compatible with their Mac, and download a notarized build without friction.

### Brand Personality

Friendly, calm, and tool-like. The experience should feel approachable without becoming playful, and technically credible without feeling dense or intimidating. It should communicate that FanBar is a focused Mac utility made with care.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, native-macOS-inspired visual language with warm breathing room, crisp typography, restrained blue accents, and subtle utility details. Prefer clean alignment, compact functional groupings, and quiet separators over heavy cards, decorative gradients, or oversized badges. Maintain the current light theme and bilingual presentation.

### Design Principles

1. Make trust and clarity immediate: compatibility, safety, source, and download paths should remain easy to scan.
2. Keep related controls and links tightly grouped, with generous separation between distinct information groups.
3. Express tool-like character through precise alignment, compact labels, and purposeful details—not visual clutter.
4. Preserve a friendly tone with comfortable spacing, soft geometry, and restrained interaction feedback.
5. Design mobile layouts intentionally: maintain readable grouping and touch targets instead of merely shrinking desktop rows.
